The Wu-Tang Clan is an American hip hop collective formed in Staten Island, New York City, in 1992.

Its members include the RZA, the GZA, Method Man, Raekwon, Ghostface Killah, Inspectah Deck, U-God, Masta Killa and Ol' Dirty Bastard.
